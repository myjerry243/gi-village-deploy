Exterior Window Casing

Make doors and windows a beautiful sight in architecture.

Product overview

WCT series casings wrap the outside of the window opening with a crisp architectural frame. Multiple depths from a slim 35 mm to a bold 250 mm profile suit both renovation and new-build façades; the ZSWCT Chinese-style casing adds a traditional ornamental option.
