Wall Coping

Sharp, durable copings for parapets and roof edges.

Product overview

YD copings cap parapets and roof edges with a crisp aluminum profile and integrated drip detail, protecting the wall below while keeping a clean architectural line. The YD-260-DT features a 10.0 mm solid aluminum plate with CNC engraving for a premium finish.
