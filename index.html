Why Gi Village

Engineered as a system, not as parts

Every Gi Village profile is designed to work with the rest of the system — so water is shed, edges are sealed and the façade keeps its clean line for decades.

  • 20+ years in the door & window industry — a 10,000 m² factory with 100+ staff.
  • Leak-proof design — system sill boards prevent water ingress at every sill.
  • Architectural finishes — durable TIGER Black, Grey, White and Coffee coatings.
  • Coordinated profiles — sills, casings, copings and canopies designed as one family.
  • Standard & custom sizes — 50–350 mm series with made-to-order options.
  • Engineered installation — detailed install nodes with structural adhesive, mortar and foam fixing.

F.A.Q.

Frequently asked questions

Are you a manufacturer?
Yes, we are a manufacturer located in Foshan, just a half-hour drive from Guangzhou. We warmly welcome you to visit Givillage.
What is your factory size?
Our factory covers a total area of 10,000 square meters and employs over 100 staff members, including 15 professional sales representatives and designers.
Can you customize colors?
Yes, we offer a wide range of TIGER color options. Custom colors extend the lead time, and a separate setup charge applies for small orders.
Can you customize dimensions?
Yes, we can customize different dimensions according to your specific requirements.
What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
The minimum order quantity for standard products is 10 sets; we accept small-batch orders for custom projects.
What is the lead time?
15 days after receiving your 50% prepayment.
What are the payment terms?
A 50% deposit is required upfront, with the remaining 50% due before shipment, payable via wire transfer (T/T).
